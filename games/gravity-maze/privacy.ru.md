# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

This Privacy Policy describes how Gravity Maze (the "App"), an Android game distributed by Curly Brackets Lab. in Ukraine ("we" or "us"), handles information. For privacy questions, contact user.sergey.b@gmail.com.

## The welcome dialog

Before proceeding with the App, the first screen of the welcome dialog provides links to the [Terms and Conditions](https://usersergeyb.github.io/games/gravity-maze/terms.html) and this Policy, and explains that the App uses advertising personalization and Analytics technologies by default, with settings that can be changed at any time. The "I have read and accept the terms" checkbox is initially off; selecting it enables "Continue". Acceptance is saved on your device. The App is intended only for users aged 18 and older, as described in the Terms and Conditions.

Select "Configure" to open the second screen, which explains the use of the advertising ID to select personalized ads with the [Unity LevelPlay](https://unity.com/products/levelplay) and [Unity Ads](https://unity.com/products/unity-ads) services, and Analytics to improve the App and your gaming experience. It contains separate "Personalize ads" and "Enable analytics" checkboxes. Both are on by default when their settings have not previously been saved. You can turn either off before proceeding and still play after accepting the Terms. "Return" takes you back to the first screen without starting advertising or Analytics before acceptance. Your choices are saved when you select "Continue".

When you update from a version without acceptance of the new Terms, the welcome dialog appears before advertising or product Analytics is enabled. Any existing personalization or Analytics preferences are preserved, including a previously saved choice to disable either. Once accepted, the Terms are not presented for acceptance on each launch or every update. An enabled checkbox before acceptance is a draft choice and does not start advertising or Analytics.

The "Privacy" button in the App's settings opens the second screen directly with your saved choices and the title "Privacy". In this mode, "Return" or the close button saves your choices and closes the dialog. These changes do not require accepting the Terms again. Closing the welcome dialog during startup exits the App without accepting the Terms or saving draft choices.

## Information you provide

You do not need to register, create a Gravity Maze account, or provide your name, email address, age, or date of birth to play. If you email us for support or a privacy request, we receive the address and information you choose to include and use them to respond to your request.

## Information stored on your device

The App stores game progress, unlocked content, settings, hint history, gameplay statistics, in-game star balance, purchase state, acceptance of the Terms, and advertising and Analytics preferences locally on your device. Google Play purchase tokens or entitlement information may be stored locally to grant purchases safely and avoid duplicate rewards.

Android backup and device-transfer features may copy the App's local progress file when supported by your device, account, and system settings. We do not operate a separate Gravity Maze account, cloud-save service, or analytics server. Clearing app data or uninstalling the App removes its local data from that device; it does not necessarily remove Android backup copies or data already processed by third-party services.

Local gameplay statistics are maintained independently of Firebase Analytics. Analytics events may include summary values calculated from your local gameplay history. The App does not send the full saved game to Firebase Analytics.

## Google Play services and purchases

The App uses Google Play services for platform features and Google Play Billing for optional purchases. Google handles payment information under its own policies. The App receives information needed to confirm purchases and grant a package of stars or the right to remove interstitial advertisements. Google may process device, network, purchase, and usage information for these services. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Advertisements

The App uses Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads and ironSource Ads for interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. Advertising services process information to request, deliver, measure, limit, and protect advertisements. Their processing is separate from the product Analytics described below.

Advertising services start after acceptance of the Terms. The App passes your personalization choice to the advertising SDKs through their GDPR consent setting: when the checkbox is off, it sends a refusal of consent and restrictive advertising settings; when it is on, it sends permission for personalization. Turning personalization off requests non-personalized advertising; it does not disable advertising services or all data processing needed to provide advertisements. The App uses settings for an adult audience, with child-directed settings disabled, and does not submit a numeric age to these services.

Depending on their settings and the device, advertising providers may process device and app information, IP address, identifiers permitted by the platform, approximate location inferred from network information, ad requests, impressions, interactions, and diagnostic data. When personalization is enabled and the identifier is available, advertising services may use the advertising ID to select personalized advertisements. Their handling of this information is described in the [Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

The purchase to remove ads disables interstitial advertisements only. Optional rewarded advertisements remain available. Removing interstitial advertisements does not disable product Analytics.

## Google Analytics for Firebase

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase after you accept the Terms and close the welcome dialog, provided Analytics is enabled. Product Analytics is enabled by default only when there is no previously saved choice; you can disable or reenable it with "Enable analytics" on the configuration screen, accessed with "Configure" in the welcome dialog or "Privacy" in settings. The Analytics preference is independent of advertising personalization. Previously saved choices remain in effect until you change them.

When you disable Analytics, the App disables Firebase Analytics collection, sends a refusal for Analytics storage through the SDK's consent setting, and stops reporting the App's gameplay events. There is no reduced Analytics mode, and the App does not queue its gameplay events while Analytics is disabled for later transmission. Local gameplay statistics continue to be stored on your device. Disabling Analytics does not delete data already collected; deletion requests are described below.

We use Analytics data exclusively to understand how this App is used and improve its gameplay, balance, usability, and technical performance. We do not use this data for advertising, personalized advertisements, marketing audiences, tracking across other apps, or unrelated purposes. We do not sell Analytics data or share it with advertisers or other parties for their independent use.

Firebase Analytics may collect an app-instance identifier, device and app information, approximate location derived from network information, app launches, sessions, and other supported automatic events. The App also sends gameplay events such as level starts, full-star adventure completions, skill-point purchases using in-game stars, and item acquisitions. Event parameters may include level and item type identifiers, training settings, attempt counts, solution lengths, and total skill points. The app-instance identifier distinguishes installations of this App; it is not your name, email address, or a Gravity Maze account.

Google processes Analytics data on our behalf under the applicable data processing terms. Our Google Analytics data-sharing options and Google signals are disabled, and the Analytics property is linked only to Firebase, without advertising product links. Google may use the data as necessary to provide, maintain, and protect the Analytics service. See [Google's explanation of Analytics data sharing](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1011397?hl=en).

Collection of the Android advertising identifier is disabled for Firebase Analytics. Its advertising storage, advertising user data, and advertising personalization settings also remain disabled. These restrictions apply to Firebase Analytics; advertising services handle data as described in the Advertisements section. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's explanation of information from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Secure data handling procedures

We use the following measures to protect the information handled by the App:

- **Private local storage.** Game progress, settings, and purchase-related information are stored in the App's private internal storage, protected by Android's application sandbox against ordinary access by other apps. Backup rules include only the local progress file. On supported Android versions, our rules permit cloud backup only when Android's required encryption capabilities are available. Device transfers and backup storage also depend on Android and your device or account settings.
- **Encrypted transmission.** Analytics and advertising information is sent through the Google and Unity SDKs using encrypted connections. Firebase documents HTTPS encryption for data in transit. Unity also documents encryption in transit for data collected by its advertising SDKs. See [Firebase security information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), [Unity Ads data security disclosures](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/grow/ads/privacy/google-data-safety), and [LevelPlay data security disclosures](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/grow/levelplay/platform/legal-resources/google-data-safety-questionnaire).
- **Limited Analytics data.** Our custom Analytics events contain the selected gameplay values described above, rather than your name, email address, or the full saved game. Collection of the advertising ID and advertising-related storage remain disabled for Firebase Analytics. Disabling Analytics stops Firebase Analytics collection and our event reporting.
- **Provider access controls.** Hosted Analytics reports are accessed through Google's account and project permissions. The App does not make Analytics reports or locally stored purchase tokens publicly available. Support and privacy requests are handled through the developer's contact email, using the information needed to respond and, where necessary, verify and locate the relevant records.

Google and Unity operate the infrastructure for the information processed through their services and apply their own security measures, as described in the linked policies and security documentation. We do not operate a separate server holding a copy of your saved game or Analytics records.

No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security. If you believe information associated with the App has been compromised, contact user.sergey.b@gmail.com. If we become aware of a personal-data breach affecting information under our responsibility, we will assess it, take appropriate steps, and notify affected people and authorities where required by law.

Data-protection rules in these countries may differ from those in your country.

An adequacy decision under Article 45 recognizes sufficient protection for the transfers covered by that decision. Ukraine does not currently have an EU adequacy decision. Where no applicable adequacy decision covers a transfer, appropriate safeguards may include standard contractual clauses (SCCs) under Article 46. For covered transfers of Analytics data between Google and us, we rely on the applicable transfer commitments in Google's data processing terms described below. The current list of recognized destinations and the scope of each decision are available from the [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/adequacy-decisions_en).

- **Google.** Google's [data-transfer framework information](https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks?hl=en) describes its reliance on adequacy decisions, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers to Google LLC and its certified U.S. subsidiaries, and SCCs where required. The U.S. adequacy decision applies to participating organizations and covered transfers, rather than every recipient in the United States. Google also describes the UK Extension for covered transfers from the UK. The [Google data processing terms](https://business.safety.google/adsprocessorterms/) applicable to our Analytics service include international-transfer commitments in Appendix 3, Part A, section 7, and links to the relevant SCCs, including clauses for transfers between Google and customers outside adequate jurisdictions where applicable.
- **Unity.** Section 9 of the [Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy) describes reliance on applicable adequacy decisions and SCCs for transfers from the EEA or UK to destinations not covered by those decisions. You can obtain information about these safeguards through the links above. Google's applicable SCC texts are available through its data processing terms. You can also contact us for further information about the transfers applicable to the App.
